          How to use LearnLoop

          LearnLoop is your personal algebra companion for Class 8 linear equations. It finds the exact line where your working went wrong, without judging or taking points off.

          Formatting Equations

          • One line per step: show each algebraic move clearly (e.g. 2x = 11 - 3 then x = 4).
          • Moving terms: remember that adding turns into subtracting when crossing the equals sign (+3 becomes -3).
          • Brackets: multiply every term inside: 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6.
          • Fractions: write answers as numbers or fractions like -3/2 or 5/4.

          Keyboard Shortcuts

          • Ctrl + Enter (or Cmd + Enter): check your notebook steps immediately.
          • Enter in practice box: submits your answer for x.
          • Esc: close this help dialog.
